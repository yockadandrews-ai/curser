## App 1: Inbound Intent AI

**One-line promise:** Sub-second scoring of every DM and email with budget/urgency signals and reply scripts.

**Target customer:** Founders and sales teams drowning in inbound

**Core problem:** High-intent leads get generic or slow replies.

**What the AI actually does:**
- Instant intent + budget scoring on every inbound message
- Custom reply generation matched to lead profile
- Routing rules — hot leads to calendar, warm to nurture
- Response time tracking and SLA alerts
- Learning from closed-won reply patterns

**Key differentiator vs 2026 alternatives:** Sub-second scoring with ready-to-send scripts, not just lead labels.

**Primary success metric:** Faster response to high-value leads and higher conversion

**Suggested pricing:** $129–$249/mo

**Liquid Glass / interaction note:** Liquid Glass UI — frosted panels, smooth micro-interactions, mobile-first
**Technical notes / integrations needed:** Email, Instagram DMs, LinkedIn, CRM
