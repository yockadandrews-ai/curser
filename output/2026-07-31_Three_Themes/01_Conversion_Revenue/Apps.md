## App 1: Offer-Optics AI

**One-line promise:** Know the market ceiling and price high-ticket services with precision.

**Target customer:** Agencies, consultants, coaches ($10k–$500k+/mo)

**Core problem:** Founders underprice because tools are built for SaaS/ecom, not service packages + live demand.

**What the AI actually does:**
- Competitor + market scanning for service-based pricing
- Lead-velocity adjustment based on pipeline heat
- Confidence-scored price bands with revenue-impact projections
- Optional Glass Slider for interactive price exploration
- Export pricing recommendations to proposals and CRM

**Key differentiator vs 2026 alternatives:** Service-specific + live lead velocity (not usage-based SaaS pricing models).

**Primary success metric:** 15–25% higher average initial contract value

**Suggested pricing:** $149–$249/mo

**Liquid Glass / interaction note:** Liquid Glass UI — frosted panels, smooth micro-interactions, mobile-first
**Technical notes / integrations needed:** CRM, calendar, public pricing sources

## App 3: Closer-Command AI

**One-line promise:** Live objection handling and buying-signal detection during the call.

**Target customer:** Founders who close their own deals + high-ticket sales teams

**Core problem:** Most tools analyze after the call; you need help while the conversation is live.

**What the AI actually does:**
- Real-time/near-real-time audio analysis during sales calls
- Whisper Text rebuttals surfaced on second screen
- Buying-signal glow indicators when prospect is ready
- Post-call coaching summary with improvement areas
- Privacy controls — opt-in recording, data retention policies

**Key differentiator vs 2026 alternatives:** In-the-moment assistance, not just post-call summary.

**Primary success metric:** 10–20% better same-cycle close rate

**Suggested pricing:** $79–$179/seat/mo

**Liquid Glass / interaction note:** Liquid Glass UI — frosted panels, smooth micro-interactions, mobile-first
**Technical notes / integrations needed:** Zoom/Meet/Teams, CRM

## App 5: Value-Verify AI

**One-line promise:** Make client results visible in real time so retention and upsells become automatic.

**Target customer:** Agencies and consultants who struggle to prove ongoing ROI

**Core problem:** Clients forget the value after the initial win.

**What the AI actually does:**
- Live "Win Cards" pushed to Slack/SMS/email
- Tracks time/money saved or results achieved automatically
- Client-facing dashboard showing cumulative value delivered
- Upsell triggers when value threshold milestones hit
- Quarterly ROI reports auto-generated from live data

**Key differentiator vs 2026 alternatives:** Continuous proof instead of quarterly reports.

**Primary success metric:** Higher retention and expansion revenue

**Suggested pricing:** $99–$199/mo

**Liquid Glass / interaction note:** Liquid Glass UI — frosted panels, smooth micro-interactions, mobile-first
**Technical notes / integrations needed:** Project tools, CRM, Slack
