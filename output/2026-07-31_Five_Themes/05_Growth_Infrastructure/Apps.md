**Core problem:** Data lives in 8 tools; Monday meetings waste an hour compiling stale numbers.

**What the AI actually does:**
- Pulls live data from Stripe, CRM, analytics
- MRR waterfall and churn cohorts
- AI narrates weekly revenue story
- Anomaly alerts
- Board-ready exports

**Key differentiator vs 2026 alternatives:** AI-narrated instant dashboard vs manual setup.

**Primary success metric:** Save 5+ hours/week, catch issues 7 days earlier

**Suggested pricing:** $197/mo

**Liquid Glass / interaction note:** Liquid Glass UI — frosted panels, smooth micro-interactions, mobile-first
**Technical notes / integrations needed:** Stripe, HubSpot, Mixpanel APIs
