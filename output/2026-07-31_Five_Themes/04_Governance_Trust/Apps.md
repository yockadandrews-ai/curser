## App 1: BrandGuard AI

**One-line promise:** Ensure every piece of content stays on-brand before publish.

**Target customer:** Marketing teams, agencies managing multiple brands

**Core problem:** Off-brand content erodes trust and wastes ad spend.

**What the AI actually does:**
- Learns brand voice and visuals
- Pre-publish review with fix suggestions
- Brand alignment scoring 0–100
- Approved messaging library
- Cross-channel consistency tracking

**Key differentiator vs 2026 alternatives:** Active gatekeeping, not static brand folders.

**Primary success metric:** 90% reduction in off-brand publishes

**Suggested pricing:** $147/mo

**Liquid Glass / interaction note:** Liquid Glass UI — frosted panels, smooth micro-interactions, mobile-first
**Technical notes / integrations needed:** CMS plugin, social scheduler integration
