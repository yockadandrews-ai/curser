Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Money Magnet Tools provides free calculators and utilities that run in your browser. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible.

Data processed on your device

Calculations, text you type, and tool settings stay on your device unless you choose to share a link. We do not require an account.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

When enabled in config.js, we use GA4 to understand aggregate traffic (page views, device type, general geography). Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

When AdSense is enabled, Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Profit Tracker page

Manual revenue entries on our optional Profit Tracker page are stored in your browser’s local storage on that device unless you connect a separate Money Autopilot API.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through your domain’s support email.
